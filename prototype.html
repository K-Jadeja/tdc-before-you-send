How this would work for real

The Date Crew's email already runs on Gmail, and the brief says profiles go to clients by email. So the tool goes where the matchmaker already is.

  1. Added to Gmail once

    As a Gmail add-on or a small Chrome extension. The matchmaker opens nothing new and logs in to nothing new.

  2. It reads two things from the draft

    Who the email is to, and whose profile is in it. Nothing else, and nothing is sent anywhere.

  3. It looks both people up

    In the client's file, and in the record of who said yes or no to earlier emails. Building that record from the old inbox is the first job.

  4. It learns from the reply

    When the client answers, the reason is filed. A new reason is suggested for the client's file; the matchmaker confirms it.

What exists today. This page only imitates Gmail, with invented people. I have not built the real add-on yet. Whether an add-on or an extension is the better route for a panel beside a draft is a question for the first week.
